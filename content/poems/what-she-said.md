The little old lady from Pasadena tapped me on my shoulder.

"What," I asked, "I don't need a car, I've already got one."

"No," she said, "It's not that, sonny, How'd you like to see my tits?"

Somewhat startled by the peculiar demands of this sweet little blue haired grandmother, gripping wicker purse.

I replied, "I'd really rather not."

But she said, "What, you some kind of fruit?"

"No," I replied "I think it's something else, I'd feel guilty."

"Fine," she said.

"Be that way. There's plenty of men in this town!"
