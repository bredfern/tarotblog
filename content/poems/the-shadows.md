Jason was lost in

the long shadows

His night vision gone

Torn from his sockets, 

Like Oedipus scream,

He'd come to sell his soul, but Satan would not buy

He'd quit the game of evil, pocketed his cash, headed

for Puerto Vallarta no more evil,

He was retired shadows drawn

back, o more evil only mediocrity
