4/1/91

Imagine me,

First child of my mother!

4/4/91

There would only be 3

laws. First, all fear

would be abolished. Next

all hatred would disappear, be-

cause no one would fear. Then

finally all greed and ambition

for power over one another

would be done away with.

Greed's got nothing to do

with money, It's all about

power. Certain people, by nature

or by upbringing, seem

compelled to gain power over

other people. The world

will never be a good, just

place, until people are
