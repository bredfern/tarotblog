3/10/91 Song of a Married Man

"This ring is heavy now.

~~Every morning it seems~~

It is gold, thick, warm.

At one time it was

very bright, even reflected the sun,

But now it is dull and

tarnished with the passing

of years.

When I wake up in

the morning and slip

it over my finger,

It seems heavier and

heavier each passing sunrise.

The earth's gravity

tugs, ~~tugs~~ and tugs, till

I fear one day it will

simply slip off."

3/12/91 For the French Girls

Your tongues are turned

upside-down, but

somehow I'm still

compelled by your

ironwood black

impossibly curled hair,

spirals and spirals

that shake nervously

under your foreign laugh.

I don't understand

a thing you say, I can't

speak French, but

I can read your eyes,

amber eyes, the

universal language of

your pupils, lit up

by the shine of your

magic brown skin,

But maybe my

tongue is upside down,

or maybe my skin

is too, too much duller

than yours, or maybe

my eyes are shut?

3/23/91

There are no words

to express this,

only pictures;

Life is a picture,

poems are artifice,

This is the electric

life, half the time

I'm unplugged, but now

It's like folk VS rock

Who needs the acoustic life?
