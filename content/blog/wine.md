3/2/91

In the end you take your stand alone. no one gives a shit. The eye of the world all fixed on self. Your lung a deep black hole in my own chest.

Why my ask a girl aphrodisiac I We swallow up into little islands of flesh, the air between us expands, till the atoms are torn apart and it seems merely a sea of ~~two~~ elementary particles and I am alone in the universe. Your eyes flicker, supernovas across these perilous distances.

**II**

Alcohol is a lubricant. We laugh and dance, I ~~love~~ take your arm, spin you around, maybe even grab a blushing breast, _(but it's enough just to touch my hands)_ You touch my face, whisper "wicked, wicked"

I could almost kiss you, but I forgot how.

_(always some blond girl all in black and drooping shoulders and hips)_

**III**

But what is it? Today my mind absorbs itself.

Why does the Pope drink wine?

(Look I even cleaned my room for you, even hid my pornography) I can't even reach your feet, the distance is too great, even the machine has been turned off, and its great blinking eyes are closed.

We read poetry, but I can barely feel the warmth of your calf.
