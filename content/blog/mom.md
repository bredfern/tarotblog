5/26/91

Son: The insanity of

the world is

driving me insane

Mom: Oh, how cute,

he's become an angry

young man.

Dad: It's just a stage, soon

he'll be just like

us. We were like

him once, all of

us, but look how

happy we are now.

Son: I think I'll go blow

my brains out now.

Hopeless Romantic: I want so much

just to ~~loose~~ lose myself

completely in another, to

merge, but there's

no depth, they just want

a lay. They suck me

in completely, take me

in, then give me nothing

of them, they ~~(she)~~

steal(s) my soul.

Cynic: All people are

basically stupid

and full of shit. All

people are just out

for themselves – they

want the basics,

comfort, security,

a good lay. It's

all "me" "me" "me"!

I used to believe

in people once. I

was stupid then.

They just fucked

me over and left

me out in the cold.

I was naive, now

I'm smart, I don't

give a shit.

The Fashion Consultant:

How could you cut your

hair? How atrocious!

Short hair isn't in,

look around you, we've

all got long hair, we

care to know! You'll

just have to grow

your hair out, wouldn't

want to look like

a marine, would you?

Or were you for

the war?

The Boss: Didn't I

tell you never to

wear that ugly shirt

on your day off?

And how could you

come in here with that

goddam earring? I told

you never to come in

here wearing them

goddam earrings, people

gonna think I got a

bunch of goddam fags

working for me.

And isn't your hair

getting a little long?

People think you're

some kinda goddam

pacenik, working at

my store!

Angry Young Man:

All I ever wanted to

be was myself. Why

can't you all leave

me alone so I can

be me. All I ever

wanted to do was live

my own life, why can't you

respect that?

Government: Hey kid, where

do you think you're

going this late, don't

you know it's past curfew?

Hey kid, what's that

you're smoking? I don't

think we approve, we'll

have to take you in.

Hey kid, what's that

you're reading, looks

obscene, have to burn

it. Hey kid, ~~looks~~

where're you parking,

that's a red zone,

have to take your

car away. Just go

home, it's safer, watch

a good cop show on

TV.

Angry Young Man:

It's just a simple

thing, all I want is

honesty. All my life,

the whole world tells

me to be honest,

then one morning

I wake up and discover

only lies.

The Church: Shut up

and go to mass,

you know the prayer,

we told it to you,

just pray it and you'll

go to heaven.

Angry Young Man: All I want is

a partner. A best friend,

someone I can share, somewhere

I can put my dick, that

don't have no callouses

for God's sake!

~~Woman~~ Girl: I've always wanted

a little boy. You'd

be nice to own.

I could put you on

my arm and parade you

around town. Want a

nice bracelet?

~~But~~ sorry, I only like

you as a friend. But

Jim!

Him: Kneel down bitch,

so I can dominate

the fuck out of you!

Aym: I'm just a nice harmless

guy, what more could

any woman want?

Running from something?

On freeway, travelling?
