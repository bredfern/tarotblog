and thrust an accusatory

finger at his brown-eyed

grandson.

"I'm sick of your

dreadlocks, boy," he said

his ancient, (lined) ~~whiskery~~

face contorted in angst,

"I'm sick of the the way

they spill from your head

like jaundice needles, like

obscene pasta, why don't

you get a haircut, goddammit!!"

He sat down slowly, ~~and read~~

tilted his head, ~~and prayed~~

painfully, ~~and watched~~ the old

scenes unfold within

the waterstained sequin

ceiling stucco.
