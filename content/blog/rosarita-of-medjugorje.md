11/21/90

Her Haiku about men:

All the ones I love

that are cold fish,

& never hate got cold hands

Hugh: Quite a girl we got here,

man oh man, look at

that Mossberg 500, what

a shine, what a shine,

Allen: What a shine,

An accidental Haiku

Handing him lit roach

He says,

Reagan was messiah
