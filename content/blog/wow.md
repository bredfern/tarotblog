11/19/90

**I**

Allen: Wow!

(shows Grace pictures excitedly)

Grace: Why don't you buy it?

Allen: I've got no money.

Grace: So, why don't you buy it—If you like it so much, why don't you buy it?

Allen: You don't understand.

(puts down book)

**II**

Father: Don't touch that, it's dirty.

(Son puts down snail that he'd tried to pick up before father intervened)

**III**

(Allen enters Grace's house, to meet her parents)

knock knock

Allen: Is anyone home

Hugh: Come right in boy, come right in!

(Hugh sits in easy chair, cleaning 12 gauge shotgun)

Allen: What's that?

Hugh: Why, it's my new Mossberg 500, bright and shiny. You like it? I got it on sale from Gun Mart, it's a real beaut!

Allen: Oh.

Heddy: You here already? Well, lets get a look at ya. Oh, jus' as I imagined! Oh, Hugh, look, he's almost as handsome as you were when you were his age. Lemme get a picture.

(Grace's mother) (enters drying bacon grease from hands, on apron) (She snaps a polaroid and returns to kitchen) Grace is nowhere to be seen.

Hugh: Sit down boy, sit down, lets have a talk, son, man to man."

Allen: (sits) OK.

Hugh: You wanna beer, you look like a beer, Hey Heddy, where's your manners? Get this boy a beer!

Allen: Uh, uh, okay, I guess.

(Heddy enters w/ frozen can of Bud Light)

Heddy: Oh, wouldn't look at 'im, oh I knew Gracie would do good someday) (just look Hugh, the picture of manhood, such a big handsome boy.

Allen: Why, thank you Mrs. Blaskowitz

Heddy: Did I tell ya 'bout when Gracie was a little girl?

Allen: Why no, Mrs. Blaskowitz

Heddy: When she was a little girl, she used to love, to smell things, everything. I remember she used to carry around a little piece a' sharp cheddar cheese in her pocket, jus' so she could take it out an' smell it, anytime she wanted. She was such a pretty little girl!

Voice! (from next room): Oh God no, no, no!

(Dull thud heard in upstairs room)
