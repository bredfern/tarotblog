11/5/90

Two trains collide

They said that four

passenger died,

miraculous,

I don't care, I put

down the paper.

"Hello class" (they do

not listen) "Mr. Shecky

could not be here today.

Just give me a hot

cuppa coffee in

my Christmas mug
