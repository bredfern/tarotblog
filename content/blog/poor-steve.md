9/19/90

Poor Steve,

Ex-rich kid

Ex-junkie

Ex-boarding school

cynic

Everyone wants it,

he said

Everyone needs it,

he said

~~but nobody really~~

wants

but really ~~no~~ nobody

wants much at all

~~even~~

even if you give it to

them.
