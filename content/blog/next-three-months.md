Here's a reading using the celtic cross layout for the next three months.

The significator is the Moon, the main theme of this time will be illusion, lies, inability to face reality, a bad dream.

The crossing card is the contrary motion here with is the two of swords, peace and balance. The regular people want peace, balance and prosperity.

The crown is the Queen of Wands, which is what we are aware of, the justice we want and the balance we seek, this is Aries in its best form, instead of blind force this is the power of justice.

The foundation represents the unconscious, and in this case it is the Emperor who represents, war, violance and chaos, the most violant and terrifying version of Aries and the opposite of what we are told we have, justice, but instead are ruled by violance.

Here the future is the crossing card, we will have peace and balance despite how bad things may be right now, in the future justice and reason will prevail over this instense time of fear and violance.

The querent is the five of wands which represents the strife we currently find ourselves under with the spread of totalitarianism and what appears to be a dismal dystopia.

The external force in this reading is the five of disks, which represents the collective worry and anxiety around the world.

Hopes and fears are represented by the hanged man, the fear of being kidnapped and being forced into slave labor in a "detention center."

The nice of cups represents the future, which is a total balance and completion and a new era of peace and happiness.
