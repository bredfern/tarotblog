9/12/90

They don't have TP

in the Soviet Union,

only newspapers.

They don't have a

Discotheque in Montana,

maybe only a marijuana

cigarette to pass around

the table in a beat

up old trailer.

They don't have black

cappuccino in

Chinatown, only bitter

yellow tea, burns

the roof of mouth

red, but keeps up
