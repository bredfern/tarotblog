11/19/90

The old man said:

"I remember, I am

one' biker girls, dat

used to pick up beer

bottles and crate

for us, no hands,

no hands."

He demonstrated

"See!"

He squatted down

till both legs

bent at 45° angles

at the knees, then

slowly straightened,

grinning

"See!"
