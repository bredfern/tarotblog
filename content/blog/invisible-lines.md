10/3/90

Sometimes I get frustrated— There's a woman being raped, down the hall she screams like a bird caught in invisible barbed wire, helpless.

But somehow I don't move to help, I remain frozen, too much work to move, the sofa is too warm, too comfortable to get up, I raise the volume on the TV, now I can't hear her scream.
