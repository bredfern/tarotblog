3/4/05

startled awake, something

about evil center

I went to roman church?

Rite: Tarot meditation

Weather: Dry, cold

Body: normal

Moon: two past last quarter

Action: Followed

recipe to pull

card, pulled "The World"

Got the idea, man is

on the same level

as the "beasts", the

woman is powerful, holds

2 wands & is surrounded by greenery

morning

12 AM –

double wands,

relaxation

Weather:

rainy

Body:

lungs

hurt, hurt

Evening

11 pm

Weather:

dry

Body: relaxed,

little asthma

Pentagram work

concentrated on

seeing the blue

light
