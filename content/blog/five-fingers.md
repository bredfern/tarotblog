10/4/90

The policeman held up five fingers in front of my face. "Count these," he said. I stared for a moment at his pale mottled middle-aged digits, then said, "One—two— three—four—five," Then I ran back into the jungle, cut myself on overhanging branches, but I didn't care, I had to get away. He was no ordinary policeman. I could still hear him calling back to me through smoke, the vines and poisonous snakes: "Come back here you, I've got to show you something" the policeman said. The policeman was an American, obviously, since he would not brave the ambiguity of the jungle. He wasn't a real cop, he was a tired man, cheap labor, the dumb God sent him.
