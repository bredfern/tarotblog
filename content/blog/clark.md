9/20/90

Clark said that Mary

was homely, but

wonderful like a

used tuxedo may

not look like new

but soft comfortable

Clark said that Mary

was homely, but

wonderful

~~He~~ Like the law of

nature the wild

youngest male feeds

last

The starving feed on

the young

He only fled wonderful

away she was young

life was male.

I can't relate

Granpa Denny was

not happy

"The Beatles why did

they grow their hair

long in '67, why not

grow it short, short

short shorter till

it recedes back to

the skull

I can't relate."

Todd stood up in the

middle of church

Sunday, said: (loud)

"You'll all be laid

by winter quarter,

you'll all get laid

I guarantee."

They did not hear

him, they said

told to go away

we don't want

you here go

home we don't

want to hear you

we don't want

to listen anymore

Todd stood

Todd is here

and he knows

by minister angel

forever
