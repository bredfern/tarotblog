They said that Kid Sataminid had a gift, He could see the hideous in all people, But this time it was different, she seemed strangely beautiful to him, though she was larger than fashionable.

He said: "Sometimes her face is shaped just like a tear, sometimes like a horse's backside, that's why I love her."
