Believe

1990-09-12

She said that she liked strawberry ice cream, and old Neal Young albums from the 1970s, but she said she was not beautiful.

She said her teeth were too big, her lips mouth was too wide, her legs were too short.

But I said, it doesn't matter, I like Neal Young, too, but strawberry only give me hives.

Your hands shake with impatience, like nervous racing-dogs, waiting for the starting gun, but I don't even know how to fall.
