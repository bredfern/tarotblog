Confessions

1990-11-05

Two trains collide.

They said that only four passenger died, miraculous!

I don't care, I put down the paper.

"Hello class" (they do not listen) "Mr. Shecky could not be here today.

Just give me a hot cuppa coffee in my Christmas mug.
