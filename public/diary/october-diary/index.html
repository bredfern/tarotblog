October Diary

1986-10-13

Well today was pretty uneventfull. The only things that happened were that I got scared out of my boots when I saw a snake. I tried to kill it, but never found it. I also got my part for drama and I'm an Irish boy.

I had a bad experience at P.E. at school, because everyone was blaming me for the losses we had. I found out that 2nd semester I will have to take an art class because Mr. Bishop is moving to Oregon.
