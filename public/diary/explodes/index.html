The electricity sizzled and popped, as the huge spark shot up through metal tires, and into the open air, exploding into a brilliant green blue flash. Wheels spun, lights flashed on, and the machine was on. The scientist sitting at the controls flipped a switch, and was instantly transported across the lab basement floor.

His colleagues stood in amazement, stunned at his accomplishment, so stunned, that in fact, they decided to pretend that the event had never happened, and so in their own stuffy Victorian ways, they dismissed it as parlor magic, and kicked Dr. Long out of the society of science for fraud.

Dr. Long packed the machine in disgust.

"Thirty years of work thrown away in an instant by a bunch of overstuffed narrow-minded cynics, who try to call themselves scientists, Rumpth!!"

He checked the coils of his machine for overheating. His machine — a machine that could travel over any given distance in an instant of time, seemed to defy all the known laws of physics, but still, it had to work, he had devoted his life to the idea of instant transportation, but his colleagues just laughed, and told him to stick to trains and horses, but he would show them.

The hate inside him grew in intensity, as he plotted his revenge.

"I can use my machine against them. They will see that I'm not an insane parlor magician. I will show them all, the world will pay for this mistake!!"

And, try tried to make them pay, but failed. The first time, he tried to dematerialize into one of his colleague's homes he dematerialized inside his former colleagues breakfast table, and died instantly.

So the world would have to wait for Dr. Long's revolutionary device to be reinvented at a later time.
