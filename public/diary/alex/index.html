Alex

1986-11-18

Later that night Alex and I stayed up until 1:00 watching movies. The next day I didn't do much except sleep and water my grandparents garden. I went to mass that night.

Today I went to mass, watered and got $25 for the week.

I've been depressed lately. The people in my gym class put me down because I'm not good in sports. Some of them call me "beastman!!"

I got my report card today. I got an A in PE, an A in Photo, an A in history, a B in English, a B in Spanish, and a C in Algebra.

I am struggling in Spanish and I hope that I can keep the B.
