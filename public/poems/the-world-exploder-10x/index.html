The World Exploder 10x

1990-09-10

The world exploder

ten times a year,

only not in a split second,

just slow like some

natural euthanasia,

not with the push

of a button, twitch

of an idle hand,

only slowly creeping

like a glacier ten inches a year,

little explosions, another

erosion of millennia,

The green turns brown,

The air turns grey,

the sea turns black,

it took her 74 years to

die, who could expect

less of a planet?
