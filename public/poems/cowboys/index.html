Commie Cowboy

1990-12-03

I was a Communist Cowboy, Ridin' the wide open range.

I always thought I was Marxist, but the others just

Thought I was just strange... your cousin Julie only eats

hamburgers when she goes out. Can't blame her, everybody loves a good hamburger."
