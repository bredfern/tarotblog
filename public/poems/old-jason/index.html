Old Jason

1990-09-20

Jason had come

to sell, capture the

riches of the world

in an instant of ecstasy

for an eternity of

pain, a memory worth

everything, but Jason

was disappointed, no

more earthly treasures,

Satan had gone to

Puerto Vallarta
