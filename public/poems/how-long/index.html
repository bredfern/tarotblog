How Long

2023-03-18

When the moon is gone from the sky and the stars themselves appear to be bleeding.

How long,, how long before I see you again?

When the moon is gone from the sky and the stars themselves appear to be screaming!

How I long,, how I long to see you again?

When the moon is gone from the sky and the stars themselves appear to disappear.

You're gone,, you're gone. Will I ever see you again?
