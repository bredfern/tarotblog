People are full of shit

1991-06-03

Everybody is so full

of shit,

but I fear there's nothing

we can do about it

why, I'm even so

full of shit, I shit

six times today,

and nothing you say,

gonna make me change,

and nothing I do gonna

make you change, I'm

afraid,

so f__k off, anyway.
