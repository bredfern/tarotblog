A Season of Insects

2026-08-18

This is the hottest time of year, and with the rising heat we're seeing a future that could be devoid of basic things like food and water.

Too much power to too few actually enables very isolated and emotionally unstable people to make harrowing decisions for us all.

I'm not a gambler, while I like music improvisation when it comes to survival and "doing life" I prefer a more moderate and balanced approach.

Not suprising with the decision made to absolutely ruin the climate now every summer its more and more biting insects, like Mother Nature herself expressing her outrage.
