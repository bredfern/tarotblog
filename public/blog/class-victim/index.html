Class Victim

2026-08-24

9/11/1990

The little boy said

that someone was at

the door, I paid no

attention,

(It's hard for the poor to pay

much attention, they're too

busy)

The man knocking at

the door back left.

He'd even come to give

me a job, too bad

for me, another missed

opportunity, and it was

my own damn fault

this time.
