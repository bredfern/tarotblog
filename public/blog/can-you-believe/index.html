Can You Believe

2026-08-25

11/5/90

George had a

vision making

peace making —

Jane screwing

Breaking arrows

Snapping hatchet

Moderating old

irons with

two little Chinese girls

at the same time

watching the

videotape play into

nothing — Oh

shit I've got

a paper due!
