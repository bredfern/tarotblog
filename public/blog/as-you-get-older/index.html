As You Get Older

2026-08-25

1/10/91

As you get older,

Gravity pulls the corners

Of your mouth down

and down.

When you're an infant

you emerge from the

womb with great

crying baby smile,

But when you die

an old man, even

The mortician can't

wipe the frown from

your face.
