For Grandfather Redfern, Doctor

2026-08-24

06/02/1991

Your house was always

full of pills

and always immaculate

elan

Grandmother was a

manic nurse

and you had a yard

full of flowers, a

hothouse full of orchids

but your voice was

always soft and gentle,

though your hands

sustained and heart restorer

And now I see you fade

mysterious before my eyes,

like the smoke from your

charcoal-patio barbecue,

and you know can outline

your body,

and let when you both happy & sad.
