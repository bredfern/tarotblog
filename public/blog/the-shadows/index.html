The Shadows

2026-08-24

9/20/90

Jason was lost in

the long shadows

His night vision gone

Torn out of from his

sockets, like Oedipus

scream,

He'd come to sell

his soul, but Satan

would not buy

He'd quit the game

of evil, pocketed

his cash, headed

for Puerto Vallarta

no more evil,

He was retired

shadows drawn

back, no more evil

only mediocrity
