October Diary

2026-08-25

10/13/86

Well today was pretty uneventfull. The only things that happened were that I got scared out of my boots when I saw a snake. I tried to kill it, but never found it. I also got my part for drama and I'm an Irish boy.

I had a bad experience at P.E. at school, because everyone was blaming me for the losses we had. I found out that 2nd semester I will have to take an art class because Mr. Bishop is moving to Oregon.

10/14/86 Today I didn't do all that much. I had a wierd math practice test during English. I talked to Amy during P.E. I found out that she is adopted and is from England. After school I went to Crown and bought my mother a book as a B-day present. Mike and I fought over how much he owed me.

10/21/86 Well, its been a while since I last wrote. Not much has happened between these dates. I got a new role in acting class, I'm an Irish idiot who gets killed at sea bringing horses to the fair. And a few tests. In Mrs. Lee's class we are starting "Fahrenheit 451." We are starting volleyball in P.E., the people on my team wouldn't let me play, though.

10/28/86 Well, it has been a few days since I've written. I haven't done much, except get ready for Halloween. This year I was invited to a party at Mike Rupp's, but I declined, because I'm party sick. Also, in Theater Theater Class we are doing our plays in a real theater. One – a real theater. Unfortunately, I don't have that big of a part, because my character gets killed early on in the play.
