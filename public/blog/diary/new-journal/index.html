80s Text

1986-10-12

Well, I decided to start a journal today. Strange things are
happening to me – I mean on Friday this Korean girl
named Amy outright tells me that she likes me, just
like that. Then this girl, Elisabeth Tuttle, who hasn't
spoken nicely to me in years, starts to go after
me, I can tell, Mrs. Lee gives us a whole list
of words we can't use when we write, screw
her. Well I'll end this entry with saying that
I'm not used to having girls go crazy over me.
