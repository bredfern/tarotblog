Not Much

1986-10-14

Today I didn't do all that much. I had a wierd math practice test during English. I talked to Amy during P.E. I found out that she is adopted and is from England. After school I went to Crown and bought my mother a book as a B-day present. Mike and I fought over how much he owed me.
