Approximation

1991-03-20

Initiation by approximation.

There are no words to express this, only pictures;

Life is a picture, poems are artifice,

This is the electric life, half the time I'm unplugged,

But now I'm plugged in!

It's like folk VS rock

Who needs the acoustic life?

It's too hollow and cold

And – great american experience
